## About Me

I am a **Data Analyst** passionate about transforming raw data into meaningful stories that drive business decisions. I specialize in building end-to-end analytics pipelines, creating interactive dashboards, and delivering actionable insights through the intersection of data engineering, visualization, and business intelligence.

- **Analytics & BI** — Power BI dashboards, KPI reporting, financial performance tracking
- **Data Engineering** — ETL pipelines, SQL optimization, structured database design
- **Python Analytics** — EDA, statistical analysis, data cleaning & transformation
- **Problem Solving** — Turning complex, messy datasets into clean, interpretable insights

> *"I transform raw data into meaningful insights, build impactful dashboards, and help organizations make data-driven decisions through analytics, visualization, and business intelligence."*
